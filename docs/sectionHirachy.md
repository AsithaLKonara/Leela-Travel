For **Leela Travel**, I’d make the landing page feel like an **interactive journey through Sri Lanka**, not a conventional travel-agency homepage.

The section order should continuously change the visual rhythm: **cinematic → editorial → interactive → immersive → conversion**.

# Visual Rhythm

The important thing is **not making every section look like a card-based SaaS website**.

I'd structure the page rhythm like this:

```text
01  VIDEO
    ↓
02  MAP
    ↓
03  HORIZONTAL JOURNEY
    ↓
04  HUGE DESTINATION IMAGERY
    ↓
05  ROUTE MAP
    ↓
06  INTERACTIVE MOOD EXPERIENCE
    ↓
07  TYPOGRAPHIC NUMBERS
    ↓
08  CINEMATIC PHOTO STORY
    ↓
09  BRAND PHILOSOPHY
    ↓
10  INTERACTIVE PLANNER
    ↓
11  FULLSCREEN VIDEO CTA
    ↓
12  MINIMAL FOOTER
```

That gives you a **rich landing page without feeling bloated**.

### The visual hierarchy I'd target

**Hero:** 100% cinematic
**Sections 2–5:** exploration
**Sections 6–8:** emotional discovery
**Section 9:** brand trust
**Section 10:** conversion
**Section 11:** emotional closing

And the recurring UI language should be:

**Poppins headings + Inter UI/body + dark glass + cyan/teal accent + huge photography + subtle grain + slow motion + thin borders + generous whitespace.**

The key is that **the user should feel like they're travelling through the website before they ever click "Plan a Journey."**
